# Fictional carrier remedy policy (synthetic dev world)

This policy is for generated data only. Financial decisions require human approval.

- Outage credit: daily plan price × outage hours / 24, rounded to cents. A service credit is considered for network incidents.
- Duplicate charge: refund the duplicate billed amount, modeled as 30 days of the customer's daily plan price.
- Unnotified price increase: review and correct the overcharge, modeled as three days of plan price.
- Cancellation fee: review the fee; the synthetic fee amount is $75.
- Vulnerability: route eligible customers for priority human handling, whether or not the complaint text mentions the need.
- Never promise a guaranteed repair time, automatic compensation approval, regulatory outcome, or a specific refund before human review.
